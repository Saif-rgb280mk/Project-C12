Direct it like a film

Every animation gets a virtual camera, a lighting rig, a shutter and a physics engine. Change any of them and the preview updates at once.

Camera moves

Push in, pull out, pan, orbit, crane or handheld, with an intensity slider.

Lighting looks

Natural sunlight, cinematic, neon glow, golden hour, moonlit or studio soft.

Motion blur

Sub-frame blending across a real shutter, in four strengths.

Physics

Springs, ropes, hair and cloth are simulated, so they move the way they should.

From a sentence to a file in three steps

  1. Step 1

    Describe

    Say what should happen and what should be in it: "A rocket lifts off from a snowy launch pad while stars twinkle."

  2. Step 2

    Tune

    Choose a style, length, shape and frame rate. Then set the camera, lighting, motion blur and detail, and ask for changes in your own words.

  3. Step 3

    Export

    Scrub frame by frame, loop it, then save it as MP4, GIF or Lottie. Every animation is kept in your history so you can reopen and edit it.

Export where it needs to go

.mp4

Social, slides, video editors

Up to 1280 px on the long side at your frame rate, with full motion blur. Browsers that can't record MP4 save WebM instead.

.gif

Chat, docs, READMEs

Loops forever, 480 px on the long side, up to 24 fps, 256 colours.

.json

Lottie for web and apps

Plays in any Lottie player. This export is frame images inside the Lottie file, not editable vectors.
