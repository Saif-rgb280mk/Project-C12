From a sentence to a file in three steps

  1. Step 1

    Describe

    Say what should happen and what should be in it: "A rocket lifts off from a snowy launch pad while stars twinkle."

  2. Step 2

    Tune

    Choose a style, length from 2 to 15 seconds, the shape (16:9, 9:16 or 1:1) and the frame rate. Ask for changes in your own words.

  3. Step 3

    Export

    Scrub frame by frame, loop it, then save it as MP4, GIF or Lottie. Every animation is kept in your history so you can reopen and edit it.

Export where it needs to go

.mp4

Social, slides, video editors

Up to 1280 px on the long side at your frame rate. Browsers that can't record MP4 save WebM instead.

.gif

Chat, docs, READMEs

Loops forever, 480 px on the long side, up to 30 fps, 256 colours.

.json

Lottie for web and apps

Plays in any Lottie player. This export is frame images inside the Lottie file, not editable vectors.
